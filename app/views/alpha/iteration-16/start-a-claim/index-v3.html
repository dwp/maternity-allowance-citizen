What you need to apply

You’ll need to upload a file or photo of:

  • your maternity certificate (MAT B1)
  • your baby’s birth certificate, if applicable
  • your baby’s still birth or death certificate if your baby was still born or had a neonatal death

If you're employed, you’ll need to upload a file or photo of your SMP1 form from each of the employers you had during the 15th week before the week your baby is due.

If you’re self-employed, you’ll need to:

  • provide the date you registered as self-employed with HMRC, if it's less than 66 weeks before your baby's due date, or
  • confirm you registered as self-employed with HMRC more than 66 weeks before your baby's due date
  • provide the date your self-employment ended, if applicable

You might be able to find the date you registered as self-employed on correspondence from HMRC or get it from HMRC.

You’ll need to have a GOV.UK One Login account. You’ll have a chance to create an account if you do not have one already.

To create a One Login account, you’ll need a file or photo of:

  • a valid photo ID, such as a passport or a driving license

Or, if you haven’t got a photo ID, you can still prove your identity with:

  • your bank or building society account details
  • your National Insurance number

All files and photos must be either a JPEG, PNG or PDF.
