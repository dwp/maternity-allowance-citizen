Support and resources during a bereavement

Advice and support

Baby loss can be emotionally and physically draining. If you are struggling, these organisations can help you understand what happened and give advice on recovery.

They can give emotional support, access to online and in-person networks and can also give legal and practical advice.

  • Advice on bereavement and loss on the NHS website (opens in new tab)
  • Baby loss information and support from Tommy’s (opens in new tab)
  • Support for bereaved families from Sands (opens in new tab)

Financial support

There is financial support available to you and your partner to take time off of work following a bereavement, and towards funeral costs.

  • Statutory Parental Bereavement Pay and Leave (opens in new tab)
  • Neonatal Care Pay and Leave (opens in new tab)
  • Funeral Expenses Payment (opens in new tab)
  • Children’s Funeral Fund for England (opens in new tab)

Process and what to do

The NHS and GOV.UK can help you understand both the medical and administrative processes that happen when your baby dies.

  1. What happens if your unborn baby dies on the NHS website (opens in new tab)
  2. What to do if a child or baby dies (opens in new tab)

These lists are not exhaustive.
