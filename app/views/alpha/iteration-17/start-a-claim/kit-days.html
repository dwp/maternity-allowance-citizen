Keeping in touch days

You can work up to 10 days while you are getting Maternity Allowance (MA) without losing any of your payments.

These are called keeping in touch (KIT) days.

KIT days let you keep in touch with your workplace or with your business if you are self-employed. You can use them to do some work without affecting your MA or you can use them to help your return to work.

If you are on maternity leave from your employer, you must agree with your employer that you can work on those days. Your employer cannot insist you work on those days.

If you are self-employed or not on maternity leave from an employer, you can choose if you want to take any KIT days.

Telling us about your KIT days

You should contact us to tell us about any KIT days that you are taking.

If you take more than 10 KIT days we might stop or reduce your MA payments.
