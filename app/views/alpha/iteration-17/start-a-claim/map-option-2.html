Starting your Maternity Allowance

You have the option to choose when your Maternity Allowance period starts.

If you do choose, the date must be between your last day of work and your baby's due date. For you, this will be between 16 August 2026 and 6 November 2026.

We'll confirm the date of your first payment when we let you know if your application is successful.
