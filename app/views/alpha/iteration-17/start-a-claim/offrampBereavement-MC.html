Sorry, you are not eligible for Maternity Allowance

You cannot get Maternity Allowance if you have had a miscarriage.

A miscarriage can be emotionally and physically draining. If you are struggling, advice and support are available for you.

Support resources

A miscarriage can be emotionally and physically draining. If you are struggling, these organisations can help you understand what happened and give advice on recovery.

They can give emotional support, access to online and in-person networks and can also give legal and practical advice.

  • Miscarriage NHS information (opens in new tab)
  • Support after miscarriage from Tommy’s (opens in new tab)
  • Support for bereaved parents from Sands (opens in new tab)
  • Support services from The Miscarriage Association (opens in new tab)

This is not an exhaustive list.

Baby loss certificate

You can get a free certificate in memory of your baby (opens in new tab) if they died before 24 weeks.

You do not have to request a certificate, and it’s not a legal document, but a way to remember your baby.

If your baby was delivered after 24 weeks of pregnancy

If your baby died, but was delivered after 24 weeks of pregnancy, or died after being born, then you can apply for Maternity Allowance.
